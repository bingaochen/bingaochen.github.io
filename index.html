Research

τ0-VLA: A Hierarchical Robot Foundation Model with World-Model-Guided Test-Time Computation
Xiaowei Cai, Yunuo Cai, Bingao Chen, Jingxiao Chen, Zhi Chen, Siyuan Feng, Tengyu Hou, Jingshun Huang, Han Jiang, Runkun Ju, et al.
Technical Report, 2026
Paper Code Model Project page

τ0-VLA is a hierarchical robot foundation model that treats high-level subtask generation as a compute-scalable inference problem: it proposes candidate subtasks, predicts their visual outcomes with a world model, and searches over the alternatives before committing. Trained on 40,115 hours of real-world data, it improves closed-loop success on long-horizon manipulation tasks spanning 13–25 steps.
